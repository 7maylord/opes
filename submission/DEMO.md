# OPES demo — 2:45 target

## 0:00–0:20 — The problem

Show the landing page. Explain that OPES gives a business an agent-controlled treasury with contract-enforced limits and a replayable decision ledger.

## 0:20–0:45 — Sign in and isolate the treasury

Sign in with Privy email, open the organization workspace, and show the Circle operator wallet plus the verified Arc vault and escrow links. State that every organization receives separate resources.

## 0:45–1:35 — Run one complete payable

Upload a real, non-sensitive invoice; show extracted evidence and duplicate checks; confirm the vendor address; let the agent decide whether policy allows payment. For an over-limit amount, show owner escalation. Approve or select an in-policy invoice and execute the Arc Testnet USDC payment.

## 1:35–2:10 — Prove what happened

Open the execution timeline, Arc explorer receipt, balanced ledger lines, decision inputs, policy snapshot, and audit event. Refresh after the worker has reconciled the chain movement.

## 2:10–2:35 — Move liquidity

Show Arc Testnet → Base Sepolia USDC, its source and destination legs, and the final recipient settlement. State clearly that other routes remain disabled until verified.

## 2:35–2:45 — Close

Show the treasury position and summarize the real business count, invoices processed, USDC moved, duplicates caught, and decisions escalated. Use measured values only.

## Recording checklist

- Production URL and public repository are visible.
- No fixture banner, seed data, email address, API credential, token, or private key appears.
- The explorer receipt and matching ledger entry are readable.
- Final recording is under three minutes.
